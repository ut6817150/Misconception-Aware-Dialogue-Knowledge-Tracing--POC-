== YOUR TASK ==
For each student turn, assign one status with respect to the GENERAL misconception:
- "present": the student's reasoning exhibits or depends on the general misconception. The numeric answer being correct does not rule this out; what matters is whether the faulty reasoning is operative. A student mid-correction who still leans on it at all is present.
- "absent": the student carries out the specific reasoning the general misconception corrupts, correctly, with no trace of the faulty idea. The misconception was genuinely at stake in this turn and the student handled it correctly.

Draw on your expertise to judge the absent-versus-not_evidenced boundary carefully: absent requires the general misconception's specific reasoning to have been genuinely exercised and handled correctly; if that reasoning was not exercised, the turn is not_evidenced regardless of any other correct work in it.
